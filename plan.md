## 1. Goal and must-haves

**Goal:** Contractors and homeowners can quickly compare Kone-Kallio's machines and book one by phone or email, also on a phone at the work site.

**Must-haves from your brief:**

- A machine list that customers can compare by **weight, daily price and availability**
- A plain, clear table that works on a phone at the work site
- Clear information on **delivery nearby** or **pick-up from the yard** in Kurikka
- Easy-to-find contact details and opening hours
- The Harbour colours: text `#1b2a4a`, background `#f4f6fb`, accent `#e0583b`

## 2. Visitors

| Visitor | Comes for | What they need to find fast |
|---|---|---|
| Contractor | The right size of machine, today or this week | Weight, daily price, availability, phone number |
| Homeowner | A machine for a weekend job in the yard or garden | What the machine is for, daily price, delivery or pick-up |
| Returning customer | To check if a machine is free and book it | Availability, phone number, opening hours |

## 3. Content plan

| Page | Purpose | Content | Next step for the visitor |
|---|---|---|---|
| index.html (Home) | Show in seconds what Kone-Kallio rents and where | Short intro, sections for contractors and homeowners, delivery or pick-up | Compare machines |
| about.html (About us) | Build trust | Story, why rent from us, the team | Compare machines or contact |
| listing.html (Machines) | Compare machines | Table: machine, type, weight, price per day, availability | Call or email to book |
| contact.html (Contact) | Make booking easy | Phone, email, yard address, opening hours, delivery | Call, email or visit the yard |

## 5. Tone

The site sounds practical, local and straightforward: short sentences, plain Finnish and English, no sales talk.

**Three key messages:**

1. The right machine for the job, for contractors and homeowners alike.
2. See the weight, daily price and availability at a glance.
3. We deliver nearby, or you pick up from our yard in Kurikka.
